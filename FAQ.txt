Q: Why is there a blank space at 2 AM on the second Sunday of March?
A: Because of the change to daylight saving time (PDT).  The hour between
   2 AM and 3 AM is skipped.

Q: So what about the switch back to standard time (PST) in November?
A: Because an hour is repeated, the "first" one is left out of the table.
   If you happen to be paddling at 1AM/2AM on the first Sunday of November,
   you'll just have to interpolate :)
   Note that the the CSV output from calculate.py will include this time
   period twice.

Q: Why is there no switch back to standard time in November 2026 and later?
A: British Columbia's 2026-03-08 spring forward was its last clock change:
   the province stays on daylight time (UTC-7) all year from then on.  The
   tables follow the system's time zone rules, so they need tzdata 2026b or
   later (the Dockerfile installs current rules).  Tables made with older
   rules show November 2026 onward, and January to mid-March 2027 onward, in
   PST (UTC-8): an hour behind B.C. clocks.
